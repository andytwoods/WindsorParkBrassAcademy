## About this funder

Brass Bands England (BBE) is the Arts Council England-funded membership and development organisation for brass bands in England. It supports member bands through the **Brass Foundations** education programme, a **Project Fund** (used as match funding against larger bids, and for crisis support), and a member **Fundraising Service** providing strategy, training, bid-writing and evaluation via a dedicated Fundraising Manager. BBE also runs the **BandSafe** safeguarding accreditation scheme.

## WFBA fit assessment

Excellent structural fit: Windsor Forest Community Brass is a brass band and holds BandSafe accreditation (a BBE scheme), which strongly implies existing BBE membership. The highest-value benefit is the **free Fundraising Service** — expert bid-writing support that directly strengthens every application in the pivoted community-band pipeline (Councillor Fund, Awards for All, Music for All). The Project Fund can provide match funding where a larger bid requires it.

The limitation: BBE is primarily a support and match-funding body, not a large primary grant-maker, so treat it as a capability and match-funding partner rather than a headline funder.

## Application intelligence

- Confirm membership first; benefits are largely member-only.
- The Fundraising Service is the standout asset — engage it before drafting the Open Doors applications so bids are professionally shaped.
- Project Fund works as match funding, so line it up alongside (not instead of) a primary bid.
