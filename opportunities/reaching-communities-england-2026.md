## Notes

Reaching Communities is the National Lottery Community Fund's core England programme. Its published focus explicitly includes tackling loneliness and social isolation — which is precisely the Open Doors narrative for an adult community band. The obstacle is scale: the programme starts at £20,001 and runs for up to five years, so it rewards organisations that can plan and evidence multi-year delivery.

For WFBA this is a "right funder, later" prospect. The sequencing is: prove the model with small local grants (Councillor Fund, Awards for All) → collect simple wellbeing/attendance/retention data → then approach a regional funding officer for a multi-year scale-up. Regional officers welcome early conversations, so a light-touch contact ahead of a bid is worthwhile once a pilot is underway.

## Eligibility evidence

"Reaching Communities... offers funding for small or large organisations, from £20,001... to strengthen communities and improve lives across England." Programme themes include building strong relationships and tackling loneliness/social isolation.
