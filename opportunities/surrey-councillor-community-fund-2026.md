## Notes

This is one of the strongest immediate local fits for the community-band pivot. The fund is councillor-allocated, so success depends on engaging the right member early and framing the project against their division's residents.

Thorpe is in Runnymede district but sits within a Surrey County Council electoral division — confirm which one (likely a Runnymede-area division) and who the sitting member is. Band members may also live across several divisions, each with its own £10k pot, so more than one councillor could potentially contribute.

Frame: an inclusive, non-competing adult band already provides safe, accredited community music; the grant funds bursary places, loan instruments, and a beginner/returner "come & try" strand so isolated, older, disabled, or low-income adults can take part. Emphasise wellbeing and belonging outcomes ("No one left behind").
