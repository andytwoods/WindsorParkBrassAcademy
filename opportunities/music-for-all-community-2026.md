## Notes

Music for All is a music-industry charity (backed by the retail/manufacturing trade) that funds instruments and equipment for grassroots music-making. The application requires an independent referee to submit a short witness statement by the round deadline.

The eligibility steer toward "deprived or marginalised areas" is the key thing to test: the Open Doors project targets adults facing isolation, low income, disability, or age-related barriers, which may satisfy the intent — but confirm directly rather than assume, to avoid wasted effort.

## Eligibility evidence

Community Project awards up to £1,500; equipment/instrument awards. Applicants "should be based in deprived or marginalised areas and be developing music education for those living in the area." Round 3 opens May/closes July 2026; Round 4 opens September/closes November 2026. Source: [Music for All](https://www.musicforall.org.uk/) and [Action Together fund listing](https://www.actiontogether.org.uk/music-all-community-project-funding), checked 2026-07-04.
