# Windsor Forest Community Brass — Funding Plan

*Prepared 4 July 2026 · for committee discussion*

## Why now

Two things have just changed that open the door to grant funding:

1. **We are now a registered charity** — Windsor Forest Brass Academy is a registered Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO), **charity no. 1218214**. Many funders only accept registered charities, so this unlocks a whole set of grants.
2. **The bank account is open** (with two signatories) — a basic requirement for most grants.

Together these mean the band can now realistically apply for several funds.

## The honest bit (read this first)

Funders rarely give money to "run a band" — a band with membership subs and paid gigs is expected to cover its own running costs. What they *do* fund is a **project with a social outcome**. So this plan is built around one:

> **"Open Doors"** — helping adults who face barriers (isolation, low income, disability, or older age) join the band, through **free/subsidised places**, a **beginner & returner "come and try" strand**, and a **pool of loan instruments** so cost is never the reason someone can't take part.

The message to funders is: *reducing loneliness and widening access to music for adults who'd otherwise miss out* — not "money for our band". One rule to remember: grants must fund **new** people coming in, not subsidise our existing paying members.

- [ ] **Music for All** — apply in the autumn round for the loan-instrument pool (check first that an adult band qualifies).

### Phase 4 — Scale up (2027)
- [ ] Once a first year of Open Doors has run and we can show it works, approach **Reaching Communities** for a bigger, multi-year grant.
